Gaza • A humanitarian story

What the war has left behind

For families in Gaza, the impact of war is not only measured in destroyed buildings. It is also measured in displacement, damaged water and sanitation systems, interrupted education, and the daily struggle to secure basic necessities.

Destroyed neighbourhoods

Homes that became rubble

Many families in Gaza have had to live in temporary or damaged shelters after their neighbourhoods were destroyed.

Displacement

Life inside temporary shelters

Families continue to face overcrowding and limited access to safe water, sanitation, healthcare and other essential services.

Children & families

A childhood interrupted

Children have lost homes, schools and familiar surroundings. Humanitarian support focuses on protection, nutrition, water, health and education.

Documented humanitarian situation: Gaza remains in a critical humanitarian situation, with continuing displacement, precarious water and sanitation services, and major constraints on winter preparations. UN assessments indicate that a large majority of structures in Gaza have sustained damage.
*Figures are dated and should be updated when newer UN assessments are published.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Which network should I use for USDT? +
Use only the network shown on the checkout page. Sending USDT through another network can result in loss of funds.
How do I confirm my transfer? +
After sending, copy your blockchain transaction ID (TXID), paste it in the checkout field and press "Confirm Donation". A receipt will appear immediately on this page.
Can I support a specific package? +
Yes. Select the package and quantity you want. The checkout summary will show exactly what you selected.
Can I donate a custom amount? +
Yes! You can enter any amount you wish in the "Custom Donation" box on the Aid Packages page.
