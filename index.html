Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know my donation reached the families? +
After your donation is confirmed, we send you a photo or short video showing the items being delivered to the family. This way, you see the real impact of your generosity.
Which payment methods do you accept? +
We currently accept USDT on the TRON (TRC20) network. This allows fast, low-fee, and secure transactions worldwide without the need for banks.
Is there a minimum donation amount? +
Yes, the minimum donation is 13 USD. This is because of network fees on the TRON blockchain. Every amount above this is welcome and impactful.
How long does delivery take? +
Once your donation is confirmed, we begin preparing and delivering the items immediately. Most deliveries reach families within 48 hours.
Can I donate anonymously? +
Absolutely. Your privacy is respected. You can choose to remain completely anonymous. We only contact you if you request a delivery confirmation.
